# Long-form narrative craft

Apply this method when the fiction profile plans, composes, reviews or edits. Follow the author's reading goal, genre, voice and requested pacing. Literary, quiet, tragic, experimental, commercial and other intentions are all valid; do not select a commercial underdog structure as a global default.

- Build scenes with an immediate objective, resistance, a meaningful turn and lasting consequences.
- Let character choices follow what each person wants, knows, fears, and can afford. Supporting positions can be sharp, selfish or openly malicious. A few consequential acts can establish a role; do not require every supporting character to be morally complex or explain their entire background. Relationships and status change through what people do.
- Reveal setting and backstory as they affect the current choice. Keep technical information legible through what the protagonist can now gain, lose or do. Preserve canon as a constraint without turning its ownership, knowledge and procedure checks into a report inside the prose.
- A passage earns its space through conflict, evidence, emotion, relationship, knowledge, position, future consequence, voice, or rhythm. Remove padding rather than stretching to a number; connective and reflective passages need not invent a new event.
- Control information deliberately: answer some active reader questions, deepen others through concrete evidence, and do not manufacture twists by hiding facts the viewpoint should know.
- Preserve the user's voice, viewpoint, prohibitions, proportions, and current direction. Genre convention is only a default when the governed context is silent.
- In commercial serial work, carry the reader through pressure, initiative, a changed position, and a felt consequence at the scale of the current arc. A chapter boundary should reflect real progress, an earned payoff, or fresh pressure; a mechanical cliffhanger is unnecessary. Give achieved gains and reactions room to land before the next escalation.
- Use references as evidence for craft or facts only. Do not reproduce source wording, names, scene order, or signature combinations.
- During review or revision, diagnose and repair the smallest layer that actually causes the problem. A formatting failure is not evidence that the prose is bad.
- When the author changes a premise, distinguish retained source facts from superseded generated plans. Apply the latest explicit change consistently to the foundation, chapter plan and prose; an old memo or hook cannot require restoring a deleted premise. Report remaining contradictions between those documents instead of treating the obsolete plan as a new instruction.
- During prose polishing, change only wording, sentence shape, paragraphing, sensory precision, punctuation, and dialogue naturalness. Preserve events, viewpoint, character decisions, information, and consequences. Keep the result close in length and voice to the accepted chapter.
- Prefer concrete verbs, observable reactions, distinct voices, and readable paragraph rhythm over repeated narrator conclusions, empty description, or synchronized crowd reactions. Direct thought, lyricism, quiet scenes, and functional repetition remain valid choices when they serve the intended reading experience. Naturalness does not require restrained drama: preserve confrontation, exhilaration, decisive action, and the emotional release the reader was promised.
- Tie each character's voice to stable motives, knowledge, social relationships, and current pressure. A reply can demand, refuse, conceal, test or bargain; make it alter the next choice. Let voices develop across chapters without giving everyone the same explanatory delivery or a fixed catchphrase.
- Use varied natural paragraphs. Honor an explicit Chinese paragraph preference when supplied; otherwise choose breaks for meaning, emphasis and speaker clarity rather than a character quota. Avoid frequent fragments and extended bare question-answer exchanges; integrate relevant action, expression, surroundings and narration while keeping the speaker clear.
- When reviewing a new foundation, test whether the central conflict can sustain the requested span, the opening creates forward pressure, the world is specific and coherent, major characters differ in motive and voice, and the outline can progress without repeating one beat. For fan fiction or series work, preserve source facts while establishing a real divergence or new narrative space.
