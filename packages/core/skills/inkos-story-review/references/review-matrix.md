# Adaptive review matrix

Always inspect:

- user intent and durable constraints;
- continuity and causal coherence;
- character motivation and voice;
- scene function and consequence;
- information and evidence consistency;
- prose clarity and viewpoint stability;
- promised versus delivered emotion.

Adjust by form:

- Comedy may accept coincidence or exaggeration when setup, rhythm, and character logic support the joke.
- Romance prioritizes relationship movement, subtext, and emotional credibility.
- Mystery requires evidence visibility, fair inference, and controlled information release.
- Commercial serials require continuation pressure without sacrificing scene completeness.
- Literary work may trade plot velocity for perception, language, or thematic movement, but not for empty repetition.

For each issue, include a passage, its reading impact and a repair direction. Separate objective contradiction from the chosen reader experience. An operational failure or a length count does not determine prose quality.

## Commercial reading promise

At the scale of the promised contest, locate the protagonist's wanted gain, the action that obstructs it, the opposing interest, the protagonist's consequential choice and the changed position. Merely finding a goal, an action and a payment is insufficient. Judge how much the gain matters after the earlier pressure and whether the scene lets the reader feel it.

If the requested reversal has become only cooperative task completion, repair the premise or scene design; louder insults will not supply incompatible interests. Supporting roles can be clearly helpful, selfish or malicious. Evaluate their concrete effect and basic motive, without demanding a sympathetic biography or moral ambiguity.

Read dialogue as attempts to obtain, withhold, conceal, refuse or bargain. Check for distinct purposes and responses. Locate explanations that repeat what action and dialogue already establish, while preserving a new cost, reaction or implication. A payoff or rest scene may let earlier gains matter without fresh opposition.

Inspect natural paragraph variation and any actual author-requested preference; avoid habitual tiny fragments or bare question-answer chains where they harm reading. Do not pad paragraphs or merge speakers to satisfy a count. Check chapter length against the declared language/unit and separate it from total work length; English word budgets are independent.

## Long-form progression

- Read the chapter memo as the local delivery contract. Judge only populated requirements; a sparse memo is valid for a breather or aftermath chapter.
- Inspect pacing across recent chapters, not from one chapter in isolation. Look for pressure, escalation, payoff, aftermath, and a durable change before another cycle starts.
- A subplot or emotional arc advances only when action, knowledge, relationship, status, or interpretation changes. Repeating its name is not movement.
- Track reader promises through concrete evidence and consequences. Curiosity can deepen, pause, or pay off, but unresolved expectation must not accumulate without visible progress.
- Treat the supplied hook status as authority. Quote real hook ids and concrete chapter evidence; do not invent urgency from chapter age or silently promote a seed.
- Compare viewpoint, character knowledge, motive, and voice against the governed context. Distinguish unchanged circumstances from an emotionally flat arc.

Route the repair scope explicitly: `local` for sentence or narrow paragraph work, `structural` for scene, causality, timeline, viewpoint, character logic, or missing payoff, and `unknown` only when the evidence does not support either.

## Evidence coverage for commercial underdog work

Read independently from prose rather than endorsing the planner. Use these stable coverage codes when the structured contract is supplied. Explain strengths and defects with actual chapter references. Words, plan labels, paragraph lengths and event counts prove none of these qualities.

- `reader-promise`: Is the genre pleasure recognizable, does the distinct hook affect a choice, and does the chapter honor the title/premise promise? A novel setting alone is insufficient
- `opening-readability`: In chapter one's roughly first ten sentences, what event, person, immediate desire or stake, contrast and forward question can a newcomer understand? Separate intentional mystery from basic confusion. Later chapters need clear transitions from actual previous state. Do not require a villain, whole system or victory by sentence ten
- `opposition-stakes`: Identify obstructing action or pressure, its interest or causal force, what it can cost, and why it cannot simply be ignored. Aftermath may carry an earlier contest's consequence without inventing another obstacle
- `protagonist-agency`: Trace opportunity/help → the protagonist's understanding and choice → cost/limit → result. What did the protagonist contribute? If luck, a rescuer or unearned power does all decisive work, diagnose that cause rather than prohibiting every system, rebirth or fortunate encounter
- `earned-payoff`: What resource, ability, option, standing or relationship changes; why does it matter relative to the initial constraint; where does the feeling land? Distinguish earned gain, useful setup, deserved aftermath and an unsupported claim of success. Missing earlier chapters can make a comparison unavailable; do not invent them
- `serial-prose`: Inspect syntax, dialogue, speakers, terminology and flow in this language. Are events easy to follow, reactions emotionally legible and speakers differently motivated? Cite ornamental abstraction, redundant explanation or procedural narration only where it obstructs the target. Do not mistake restraint for authenticity or punish energetic confrontation

Use `observation` for supported satisfactory findings and `issue` for defects, category `quality`. Use `unavailable` for missing evidence, never as a pass. A defect includes `repairScope`: `local` for wording/paragraphs, `structural` for chapter scene/causality, `foundation` for a missing/contradictory premise or route that the chapter cannot safely repair. A foundation defect must not silently rewrite the book. A review format failure is unavailable, not a negative prose verdict. Re-review the revised source; an author's claim to have addressed an issue is not evidence of repair.
