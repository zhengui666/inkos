# Short-fiction production checklist

Apply the commercial conflict and payoff checks when that is the requested reading goal or the open default. An explicit literary, quiet, tragic, experimental or other author intent governs its own choices; preserve agreed facts and the authorized revision scope.

## Before writing

- For a new Chinese chapter with no different agreed target, plan roughly 2000–2500 non-whitespace body characters including punctuation, excluding the title. This is a chapter budget, not the total length of a short work. Preserve explicit or already saved targets during continuation. English uses its own declared word budget; never silently reinterpret the Chinese numbers as English words.

- State the protagonist's immediate desired gain and what they can lose. In the default commercial turnaround, make the opposing interest legible through an action that actually limits their options. Name who benefits if they fail. A chain of tasks or polite agreements is insufficient when the promise is a contested reversal.
- Give the opportunity a concrete source. An ability, useful skill, relationship, discovery or coincidence can open a route; the protagonist must choose how to use it, provoke or overcome resistance, and cause the result.
- Establish supporting positions through consequential behaviour: withholding, taking, deceiving, favouring, helping or defending. Basic desire and causality are enough. Clear malice needs no sympathetic biography, and verbal insults alone do not establish effective opposition.
- For the default commercial web-fiction target, the opening makes the protagonist's goal, immediate obstruction, and opposing interests legible through an action or exchange. Give the reader a reason to take an interest in the protagonist's next move. Follow a different opening mode when the author explicitly chooses it.
- Name the specific return the reader is waiting for and build its importance before the win. Money should relieve the established pressure, a position should confer usable rights, and a relationship change should affect treatment or choice. A routine payment or praise alone is not proof of a meaningful turnaround. Escalation changes options, cost, advantage or relationship rather than repeating the same humiliation louder.
- Evidence and setup exist before each major reversal, if the story uses reversals. Surprise should change the meaning of earlier facts rather than invalidate them.
- When timestamps or quantities carry decisive evidence, establish one reference timeline and identify what each record measures. Apply any clock offset consistently, then check that every alleged cause still precedes its effect under the same conversion.
- Ending pays off the central conflict and emotional promise at the requested short-story scale. Show the decisive action, its result, and enough reaction for the reader to feel the gain or release. For an explicitly chosen tragic, literary, or open ending, closure does not require a victory, moral, or last-line twist.

## During whole-story review

- Check the central contest in the actual manuscript: what does the opposing party do, which choice does that force, how does the protagonist change the outcome, and how much does the gain matter after the established pressure? A named goal, busy actions and an announced success do not by themselves answer those questions.
- Distinguish a premise that lacks the requested opposition from a weak sentence. In an authorized structural repair, change the incompatible interests, options or consequences; harsher dialogue cannot repair a cooperative task masquerading as a contest. Do not silently change canon during a scoped polish.
- Check whether explanatory passages change what a character or reader understands, feels or can do; compress repeated explanation without removing necessary context.
- Later chapters do not collapse into summary.
- Chapter transitions preserve causality.
- Names, facts, objects, and evidence remain consistent.
- Calculate ages, generations and elapsed years together; a family relationship must be possible on the same timeline as school, work and historical events.
- Check recurring objects for incompatible locations, holders or uses across the actual scene sequence. Distinguish a contradiction from an ordinary action omitted between scenes. Require added explanation when the omission breaks a decisive cause, character choice or payoff, rather than for every routine movement or technical operation.
- For Chinese prose, generally use natural paragraphs around 20–80 characters, varying their length without frequent isolated fragments. Blend an ongoing exchange with relevant action, expression, environment and narration, while keeping speakers distinct. Avoid bare question-answer chains and gesture-per-line padding. Apply English paragraphing in its own natural syntax, without transferring Chinese character counts as English word quotas.
- Title and synopsis promise only events the manuscript actually delivers.

## Packaging

- For amounts, quantities, timing and responsibility, use the manuscript's final evidence-backed conclusion. Earlier estimates and a character's suspicions are not established outcomes. If a precise number cannot be verified, describe the conflict and payoff without inventing or inflating it.
- Ground sales claims in scenes actually present. Do not market an unresolved review finding as a proven strength or promise unverified quality. Describe the story's concrete conflict and payoff.

## Revision

- Start from the author's revision request. Preserve the title, conflict, ending and scene functions that the author has not asked to change.
- Identify affected chapters and a shared correction plan before revising. Keep ages, generations, dates and object transfers consistent across them.
- Preserve causal setup and meaningful handoffs. Repair the actual gap and carry it through affected transitions. A fact that stays unchanged need not be restated merely to demonstrate compliance with the plan.
- Judge the whole manuscript against its creative intent; separately verify the latest requested repairs. Label each finding as an issue, a resolved prior issue, or a neutral observation and give concrete evidence.

## Before submitting chapters

- Match each submitted chapter to its own plan: opening state, scene function, newly discovered information, and final beat. The full outline supplies future context; later investigations, confrontations and revelations must stay in their assigned chapters. Do not replay an already completed investigation in the next chapter.
- Fit the requested chapter length by choosing the planned scene's essential actions and exchanges. Complete that scene before stopping; a complete chapter does not need to enact the whole story. Remove repeated reasoning or future chapter events before shortening the scene into a synopsis.
- Read the final paragraph of every submitted chapter. Complete its utterance, action and scene transition; do not submit text that breaks off inside a sentence or dialogue. A continuation hook must be a complete beat.
- After any output interruption, reconstruct the complete affected chapter from the plan and surrounding chapters; do not carry a truncated excerpt forward as a finished chapter.

- For a scoped revision, first extract binding facts from the unchanged chapters into the revision brief: participant identities, relationships, who owns each decisive document, event order and later consequences. Check the replacement against these facts before submission; do not introduce a new participant role that contradicts a later unchanged scene.

## Flexible pacing, not platform thresholds

For the default commercial web-fiction target, strong opening opposition, protagonist agency, changing information, emotional pressure, earned payoff, and mobile readability are the creative priorities. An explicit alternative author intent takes priority. Fixed opening lengths, timed hook intervals, and dialogue percentages are optional working preferences, not universal requirements or verified platform algorithms. Apply such numbers only when the current author explicitly requests them. Do not infer a hard quota from a genre or platform name.

Measure information gain by what changes for the reader or character: a choice, constraint, relationship, piece of evidence, reinterpretation, or emotional understanding. A quiet scene can build anticipation or make a reward felt; a string of incidents can still repeat the same position. Choose the pleasure for this genre: competence, justice, reversal, romantic progress, discovery, survival, or another promised gain. Use credible opposing interests and active choices; revenge, humiliation, crowd applause, and cliffhangers are not required forms of payoff.

## A scope-aware production loop

Use the relevant steps within the current authorized operation: clarify what makes the premise and stakes specific; compare title or opening approaches while those choices remain open; build a causal event skeleton; draft the planned scenes; review rhythm, causality, characters, and closure; then repair the identified passages. Skip settled decisions and unnecessary steps. Do not replace an accepted title or opening, or restart the whole story, merely to complete this sequence.

An opening comparison should contrast concrete reader questions, character choices, and promised consequences, not copy another story's setting or surface phrasing. Judge a candidate against the actual story it introduces. A model's predicted completion rate is not reader evidence.

At paragraph level, identify the useful movement of action, perception, information, emotion or voice. When an action and reply already make the meaning clear, remove a subsequent explanation unless it contributes a new cost, reaction or implication. Paragraphs can connect or let a moment breathe; each paragraph need not contain a new event or cliffhanger. During whole-story review, distinguish a missing causal setup from a wording problem, and keep proposed plot changes separate from prose polishing. Recheck the ending and affected setup against unchanged chapters after each accepted repair.
