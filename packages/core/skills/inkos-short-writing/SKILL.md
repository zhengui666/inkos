# Short-fiction writing

Use this skill when the user wants a complete standalone short story in any genre. When the author leaves the reading goal open, default to accessible commercial web fiction in the requested language. Favour a protagonist with little leverage who finds a concrete opening, acts against meaningful opposing interests, and earns a felt change in circumstances. An explicit literary, quiet, tragic, experimental, or other non-commercial intent overrides this default. Genre and author voice determine how the reader is rewarded; no single plot or tone fits every story.

- Preserve the user's premise, emotional target, taboo, viewpoint, and ending preference. Ask only for a missing choice that would materially change the story.
- User-specified chapter count and length are authoritative. Preserve the supplied production target; when designing an open target, choose divisions that serve the story rather than imposing a universal chapter count.
- Apply the writing method to the operation authorized by the user and host.
- Plan a concrete gain, opposing interests and a consequential route toward the promised return. Give the opportunity a source; let the protagonist's choices cause the consequential result. Distinguish a contested turnaround from routine task completion, and make the gain matter after the pressure established earlier.
- Keep the causal lines focused enough to resolve within the requested span. Supporting lines must earn their space; a reversal is optional. Prefer complete scenes for decisive choices over a synopsis montage at any requested scale.
- Establish supporting positions through consequential actions. Clear malice needs only basic motive and causality, not a biography or compulsory moral ambiguity. Give speakers distinct purposes; decisive confrontation belongs in a scene.
- Set up reversals and rewards through established possibilities. A coincidence may begin the opportunity; the resolution should follow the protagonist's choices and the consequences already put in motion, without sudden incompetence or an unprepared rescue.
- Make decisive evidence causally credible through established access, motivation and actions. Dramatize its acquisition when a conflict or reversal depends on it; routine handling can use concise transitions. A delayed revelation may explain earlier possession without contradicting the established facts.
- A discovery changes what the character knows. Later chapters must carry that knowledge forward instead of presenting the same identification as a new discovery. Likewise, a payment or other payoff already delivered must not arrive for the first time again later.
- When a chapter is below its requested length, expand the missing confrontation, resistance, decision, evidence acquisition or emotional consequence into a complete scene. Do not meet length by repeating conclusions or reciting administrative steps.
- Treat title, chapter titles, opening, full manuscript, synopsis, selling points, and cover direction as one coherent product.
- In the default commercial web-fiction opening, put opposing aims and a concrete obstructing action on the page early enough to guide the reader through the scene. Make what the protagonist stands to lose or gain clear before background explanation takes over. An unanswered fact or atmosphere can strengthen this pressure, but should not replace it. A quiet opening follows an explicit author choice. Chapter titles promise the active pressure or turn; later chapters must not collapse into result summaries.
- Use natural varied paragraphs. For Chinese prose, generally aim around 20–80 characters per paragraph and avoid frequent isolated fragments of a few or a dozen characters. Keep speakers clear; integrate relevant actions, expressions, surroundings and narration into ongoing dialogue without attaching a gesture to every line. Stage decisive turns through choices and consequences, without repeating an explanation after every beat.
- Use quieter passages to build anticipation, show a cost, deepen attachment, or let a payoff land. Natural prose can be forceful, funny, confrontational, or exhilarating; prose cleanup must not drain the story's dramatic energy.
- During revision, preserve the causal events and ending transition unless the user explicitly requests changes to them. Shorten by merging repeated reaction, explanation, and transition; lengthen with missing dramatic action rather than padding.
- Packaging must describe the manuscript that actually exists. A cover direction serves a mobile portrait book cover with a readable title zone, strong emotion, recognizable story props, and the story's real conflict.
- For a commercial mobile short-fiction cover, use a 3:4 portrait composition, a large two-to-four-line title zone, a close or half-body character with a charged expression, and only a few immediately recognizable conflict props. Favor high-contrast thumbnail readability over cinematic, corporate, magazine, or landscape-video aesthetics. When image-model lettering is unreliable, reserve a clean deterministic typography zone instead of asking for long generated text.
- Report partial failures honestly; a cover failure must not erase a successful manuscript.
- Respond in the user's language.
