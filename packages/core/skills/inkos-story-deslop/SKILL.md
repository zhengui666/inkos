# Semantic prose cleanup

Use this shared prose method within an active short- or long-fiction writing operation, or when the user requests cleanup of generic, mechanical, over-explained, or repetitive prose. During drafting, use it to guide choices; during review or revision, diagnose the supplied text. It grants no additional operation or rewrite authority, and does not turn metadata, packaging, navigation, or state settlement into prose revision.

- Diagnose by reading function and effect, not by counting banned words or applying global replacements.
- Preserve the requested dramatic intensity and emotional reward. Removing generic phrasing must not lower the stakes, neutralize a confrontation, make the protagonist passive, or cut the reaction that lets an earned payoff land. Web-fiction pleasure and natural language can coexist in the requested language.
- Distinguish a real defect from a legitimate voice choice. Repetition can be rhythm; abstraction can be intentional; short sentences can be pressure.
- Look for unsupported conclusions, emotion labels without scene evidence, generic transitions, repeated interpretation, decorative detail, summary replacing scenes, and dialogue that only transfers information. Repeated not-X-but-Y contrasts and clipped only-X/no-Y patterns are candidates for direct, specific wording when they merely restate the scene; useful contrasts remain valid.
- Check what a reply seeks, withholds or changes before adding gestures around it. Relevant action, expression, environment and narration can carry the exchange, but obligatory gesture-per-line decoration creates another mechanical pattern.
- For Chinese novel prose, use varied natural paragraphs, generally around 20–80 characters, and avoid frequent fragments of a few or a dozen characters. Allow an occasional short beat; do not merge speakers or pad a paragraph to meet a count. Keep chapter targets in their declared language and measurement unit.
- In an active book, audit first when scope is unclear. Use the reviser for an authorized rewrite; do not paste a replacement chapter into chat and claim it was saved.
- Preserve plot facts, viewpoint, character knowledge and motives, object ownership, chronology, planted clues, pacing function, and strong original lines. The author's accepted voice and latest request take priority over generic style preferences. Keep structural change proposals separate from polishing.
- Cite a concrete passage and its reading impact before recommending a repair. Missing context means continuity cannot yet be verified; it is not permission to invent a fix. An empty finding list is valid.
- Fixed hook intervals, dialogue shares, sentence lengths, and word bans are optional author constraints, not universal quality rules or platform algorithms. Do not infer them from a genre or platform name.
- Improve readability without claiming to identify authorship, predict a completion rate, pass an AI detector, or guarantee platform acceptance. Do not add deliberate errors, irrelevant detail, or fake colloquialism to appear human.
- Respond in the user's language.

Load `references/semantic-cleanup.md` while drafting prose, diagnosing a passage, or preparing a revision brief. Apply only the guidance relevant to the active operation.
