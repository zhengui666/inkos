# Web-fiction method update

The two novel profiles default to accessible commercial web fiction in the requested language. An author's explicit literary, quiet, tragic or other intention takes priority. The shared method now connects a concrete opportunity to the protagonist's choice, effective opposition, a cost or change of approach, and a consequential return. Supporting positions may be sharply good or bad; basic motivation and causal behaviour suffice without compulsory moral ambiguity or a biography for every supporting role.

The manuscript must show the requested reading experience. Merely finding goal, action and reward labels, routine cooperation or an announced success does not establish a contested turnaround. Review identifies the faulty layer and keeps changes to events separate from prose polishing. Cleanup preserves dramatic intensity and useful repetition while reducing generic contrasts, repeated explanation and uniform dialogue.

Chinese prose generally uses naturally varied paragraphs around 20–80 characters, with occasional exceptions. Ongoing dialogue incorporates relevant behaviour and narration without a gesture quota or merged speakers. This is a reading preference, not a rejection threshold or platform rule.

## Length defaults and saved work

- New Chinese short work: 5 chapters, target 2400 characters per chapter, approximately 12000 in total. The chapter target falls within the requested approximate 2000–2500 range; it is not the entire short work's length.
- New English short work: the existing 12 chapters at 650 words remains unchanged.
- New long work: Chinese target 2400 characters; English keeps its independent 2000-word default. CLI, Core and Studio now agree on the language-aware fallback.
- Explicit and saved targets keep priority. Existing manuscripts are not migrated. The CLI short revision command no longer supplies an implicit 1000-character target or a fixed model that overrides project settings.
- Existing hard length contracts retain their semantics. These preferences introduce no paragraph quota, blanket new hard minimum, or conversion between Chinese characters and English words.

## Validation boundary

Prompt-assembly tests cover both novel profiles, actual worker names, native and compatible clients, explicit author overrides and non-novel isolation. Fake-model production tests exercise real target normalization and persistence; CLI tests inspect the arguments reaching the existing tool and pipeline. These tests verify the instruction and routing contract. They do not establish improved prose, reader enjoyment, detector performance, publishing acceptance or revenue. Those outcomes require reading actual new text against the intended experience.
