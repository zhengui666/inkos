# Author intent and reader promises

A reader promise is optional creative context, not a mandatory commercial contract. Preserve explicit author intent, source canon, tone, genre, required events and prohibitions in `Work.plan.brief` and appropriate `Work.domain` data. Imported legacy `readerContract` data is retained without imposing it on other Works.

Distinguish established events from plans, future intentions and inferred possibilities. Setup, aftermath, quiet scenes and intentional open endings can be valid. A chapter count or a phrase such as “The End” does not prove story closure, and the core does not require an ending proof.

Official profile instructions and replaceable craft Skills guide composition. Review identifies author requirements, contradictions and ordinary suggestions; only the first two trigger configured necessary revisions. Retain a draft on model failure. Exact version/policy reviews are reusable; change the policy ID when changing its meaning. Do not use keyword counts, model-predicted popularity or platform acceptance as literary evidence.

Author changes to a plan affect subsequent relevant steps. Saved content remains a stable artifact revision, and changes to submitted/published content require an explicit distribution update. Editorial review, submission, platform review and publication are separate outcomes.

See [runtime operations](runtime.md) and [craft provenance](narrative-craft-guidance.md). Historical mandatory commercial review receipts and manual frozen publishing packages are not current InkOS execution paths.
